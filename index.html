About

I'm a software engineer at Meta DevInfra in New York, where I work on infrastructure for AI coding agents: the tools, protocols, and verification loops that let an agent run on a developer's machine and check its own work.

I have spent more than six years at Meta building products and infrastructure across the stack. I started on Meta Business Suite, moved to content creation for creators to work on Facebook's video creation experience and early GenAI creator tools, then to Partnership Ads on the ads side, and most recently to developer infrastructure.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Brandeis University.
